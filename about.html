About

Emanuel Moreno recieved their Bachelors of Science in Biological Sciences at the University of South Carolina. He is currently working at a water testing laboratory within Columbia, SC as a Laboratory Analyst. His work primarily involves museum and performing arts photography, but he is also exploring opportunities in wildlife and portrait photography.
